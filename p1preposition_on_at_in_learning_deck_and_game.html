Where things are: AT vs IN

Let's learn the secret rule for general locations versus being completely inside!

General Location: AT

Use AT to describe the general location of where someone or something is. It answers the question: "Where are you at?"

Examples:

🏫 Mary is at the school.

🏠 John is at home / at work / at the park.

Inside Building: IN

Use IN when you need to emphasize that someone or something is strictly inside the building or area.

Examples:

🏫 Mary is in the school (inside the building).

📚 The book is in the library / in the room.

On a Surface: ON

Use ON when something is resting on top of a surface.

📦 on the box 📚 on the shelf 🟩 on the floor
🚌

Transport Rules: IN vs ON

How do we travel? The rule depends on the size of the vehicle!

Use IN for Vehicles

Smaller, personal, or enclosed vehicles where you must sit down and cannot walk around.

🚗 in a car
🚕 in a taxi
Use ON for Vehicles

Larger, public, or open vehicles where you can stand, walk, or stand on the surface.

🚌 on a bus
🚆 on a train
🚲 on a bicycle
